# Sources and licences

`ui-craft` is an adaptation. The rules in SKILL.md are rewritten in our own words from the sources below, condensed,
and overridden by Ultimate's theme, density and sentence-case conventions wherever they conflict. Nothing else was
copied: no palettes, fonts, scripts or design-direction steps.

| Source                                                                                                 | Licence    | What we took                                                                                                                                                                                                | What we changed                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop) by Damir Sokolovsky ([@heydamir](https://x.com/heydamir)) | CC BY 4.0  | The slop-signs checklist (`references/signs.md`)                                                                                                                                                            | Kept the app-UI groups; dropped direction, palettes, fonts, heroes and visuals; fixes point at Ultimate's tokens |
| [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) by Paul Bakaus                                     | Apache-2.0 | The "operate" (app UI) rules from `reference/operate.md` and `craft-floor.md`: restrained accent, the state vocabulary, skeletons over spinners, no decorative motion, no modal by default, no nested cards | Summarised; dropped the persuade mode, `PRODUCT.md`/`DESIGN.md`, commands, detector and live mode                |
| [Web Interface Guidelines](https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines) by Vercel          | MIT        | Accessibility, focus, forms, truncation, `tabular-nums` and `transition-all` rules                                                                                                                          | Sentence case instead of its Title Case rule; dropped rules that do not apply to panels                          |
| [shadcn skill](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/tree/main/skills/shadcn) (`rules/styling.md`) by shadcn | MIT        | Semantic tokens only, `gap` over `space-*`, `size-*`, no `dark:` colour overrides, layout-only `className`                                                                                                  | Mapped to Ultimate's own tokens and hand-kept primitives; dropped CLI and registry rules                         |
| [Emil Kowalski's skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills) (`emil-design-eng`, `break-ui`)       | MIT        | Animation by frequency, durations, no `ease-in` or scale from 0, worst-case data testing                                                                                                                    | Condensed to a few lines                                                                                         |
| [ibelick/ui-skills](https://github.com/ibelick/ui-skills) (`improve-ui`)                               | MIT        | The evidence-gated audit: the repo's tokens are the authority, every finding needs a rule, evidence and one correction, findings capped                                                                     | Our cap is eight; the render comes from `verify-ui`                                                              |
