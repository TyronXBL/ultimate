The line under the composer (`composer.status`) shows the conversation's cwd/workspace and git branch, `↑input ↓output R cacheRead W cacheWrite`, cache hit rate, cost (`(sub)` on a subscription), and context use of the model's window (warning above 70 %, error above 90 %). Hover for per-model/tool usage and context details. The models extension's composer toolbar shows the model and thinking level.

The bottom status bar is global: model output tokens per second across all conversations over the last 30 seconds, a live running-agent count including subagents (visible at zero), project-wide settled input/output tokens and cost, and the server connection with its generation. Hover the rate for input and cache rates. Provider reports may arrive late, and tool usage is excluded from rates but included in spend. Other extensions contribute the build.

Hovering a stored message shows its context and cost on its action row (`messageDetails`): a response's context as a share of its model's window and its cost; the last response of a turn adds its model, the turn's duration since the input, and the cost of every response in the turn; an input shows the context it was sent into. The tooltip breaks down the response, the turn, the context, and the model with its thinking level. A turn whose input is not loaded yet shows the response alone. `turns.ts` holds these transcript helpers.

What a branch cost shows as a quiet chip wherever a branch is named: in the header (desktop), on its sidebar row's work line, on its idea card and on its landing cards. It is the API-price dollars of every model call in the top-level conversation and everything it owns at any depth (workers, reviewers, designers, their subagents); a tree whose models have no price shows tokens instead. Its popover lists tokens and dollars by provider and model, and "no price" for a model without one (a custom model with zero cost, a free one), so $0 never reads as free. A fork starts its `pi.usage` empty, so a branch never counts its root's spend; preview instances keep their own store and are not counted. No share of a subscription's limits is shown: providers report one account-wide percentage that other clients share. The `trees` action returns every tree's spend from the same scan as `rates`, with each conversation's owner kept from its records; `spend.tsx` reads it once for every chip, again when a run starts or ends and every 15 seconds while one runs. Sidebar, workspaces and ideas render it through the `conversations.spend` point, so they import nothing of this extension.

Conversation totals come from `pi.usage`, the model from `pi.agent`, and cwd, branch, and process from system info. The context meter reads the shown conversation's `conversations.context` document, maintained server-side by conversations: measured prompt tokens after an answer, or an estimate after compaction/reset until the next answer. It keeps the same percentage, warning colours and estimate tooltip without a browser estimate action. Historic per-message windows still come from `GET /api/models`. Shared context helpers live in `lib/context-usage.ts`; `context.ts` keeps spend totals. The `rates` action also returns settled totals from every conversation's `UsageDoc`; it scans once on open and updates from commits, independently of the shown conversation.
